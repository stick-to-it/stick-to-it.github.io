Stick To It

To-do lists on popsicle sticks. Hold to add one, slide it over when it's done.

Scroll
What it is

Every to-do is a popsicle stick on a board. Write on it, slide it across when it's done, and feel that little click. A board for every day of the week, or start blank and make your own.

What it does
Share a board, liveFamily, roommates or your team on the same boards at the same time.
RemindersA stick can nudge you at the right time, and repeat daily, on weekdays or weekly.
Home-screen widgetToday's sticks on your home screen. Tick them off without opening the app.
Search everythingFind any stick on any board, on any day.
Make it yoursCork, chalkboard, marble, blueprint, living galaxy, lava, rain and more. Your font, your colours.
The whole weekTurn your phone sideways and every day sits side by side.
