Stick To It - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026. Stick To It is made by ShakiiART & Meowz Network Productions.

What stays on your phone

Your boards, sticks, settings and reminders are saved on your device. We don't see them.

Shared boards

If you share a board, its contents and the display name you choose are stored in Google Firebase so the people you share it with can see and edit it live. Only people with the board's share code can open it. Stop sharing or delete the board to remove it.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads. You can reset or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads. See how Google uses data for ads. Buying Stick To It Pro removes ads.

Purchases

Upgrades are bought through Google Play. We never receive your payment details.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: contact us through the developer email on the Google Play listing.
